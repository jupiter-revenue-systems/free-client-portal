# Update Prompt

Use this every time the work moves: a stage changes, something is finished, or you need something from the client.

Paste the prompt, then the current page, then 1 or 2 rough lines about what changed.

```
Here's my client's current progress page, and below it what changed today. Update the page. Move finished things to Done so far. Rewrite Where we are in one sentence. Refresh What's next and What I need from you. Change the date at the top to today. Keep the same 5 blocks and the same plain voice. Then give me a 2-sentence message I can send the client that says what changed and links them to the page.

CURRENT PAGE:
[paste]

WHAT CHANGED:
[1 or 2 rough lines]
```

## Before you share the update

- Read it once, top to bottom.
- Check every date.
- Check every "done". Did you look at the finished thing yourself?
- Check that What I need from you has one clear ask, or says nothing is needed.
